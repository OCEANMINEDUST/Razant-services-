Our Story

Razant Services Limited is a wholly Nigerian-owned oilfield services company committed to excellence in project delivery and human capital development. With operations primarily in the Niger Delta and beyond, we provide end-to-end EPIC services to major operators, marginal field owners, and indigenous E&P companies. Our team of experienced engineers, technicians, and trainers ensures every project meets the highest standards of quality, safety, and compliance with Nigerian Content Development requirements.
